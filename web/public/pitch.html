05 — ARCHITECTURE

How it works · one flow, three phases

Encrypted in. Aggregate out. Encrypted back.

commit-private · dual encrypted handle batch-execute-public-aggregate · sums only decrypt claim-private · encrypted × public scalar
Architecture — 30s Three phases. Encrypted in: your side seals inside Intel TDX and lands as two handles. Aggregate out: at epoch close ONLY the sums decrypt — that's an on-chain invariant, greppable in CI — and route through the real FPMM in one batch. Encrypted back: you claim in confidential cUSD. Plaintext exists at exactly one boundary — the market itself.
